About

The person behind every stage.

Before design, I worked in roles where communication and empathy mattered most, and I kept asking: why is this confusing, and how could it be simpler? That question took me to New Media Design at Jönköping University.

Since graduating in 2025 I have worked across AI SaaS, smart hardware, data products, accessibility and consumer audio. Since August 2026 I have done marketing, content and digital design at Transparent in Stockholm.

Outside work, I'm a mother of two. We travel often, and I am usually testing a new AI tool to see how it thinks.

  • I solve fastIdea to a winning product in three hours, alone.
  • I drive myselfBuilt my own AI agent because explaining the same context twice was a waste.
  • I learn quicklyTools change; judgment doesn't. I learn the pattern, then pick the right tool.
  • I care about precisionMy AI images pass review by a team of designers, or they don't ship.
