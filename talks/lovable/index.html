    What I learned

    What story should be told? +

    Individual placement inside micro/macro structures.

    What should be simplified? +

    Mapping extensive data into clean visual coordinates representing human concerns.

    What should be avoided? +

    Averaging qualitative differences with aggressive scoring patterns or negative grading.

    How should the user feel? +

    Informed, space-to-breathe, validated, and quietly prompted to evaluate work boundaries.
